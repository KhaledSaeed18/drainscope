One idea, across every surface.

Room to breathe

Leave at least one quarter of the mark’s nominal height around the entire lockup. At a 96 px mark height, that is 24 px on every side.

Minimum digital widths: 180 px for the horizontal lockup, 48 px for the full mark. Use the dedicated symbolic mark at 16–32 px.

Keep the shares intact

Use supplied primary, dark, monochrome, or white files. Preserve the shape, proportions, order, and spacing.

Use a quiet contrasting background. Avoid stretching, rotations, extra outlines, drop shadows, or substituting a lightning bolt.

Signal, with restraint.

Teal identifies the brand; cyan supports dark surfaces. Text and actions use theme-specific tokens rather than applying one swatch everywhere.

Voice and naming

Use drainscope in the wordmark, project name, and commands. Use sentence case for interface copy.

Lead with the user’s question: “What used my battery?” Explain measured energy and estimated shares separately.

State hardware support and limitations plainly. Describe what users can inspect, rather than promising automatic savings.
